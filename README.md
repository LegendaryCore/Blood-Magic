## 📖 What Is Blood Magic?

**Blood Magic** is an arcane art practiced by mages who gather vast power through a forbidden material: **blood**. Every action performed with this volatile magic can prove deadly — you have been warned [citation:5].

Progression runs through **six Blood Altar tiers**, each unlocking stronger orbs, sigils, and rituals. You start by cutting yourself with a dagger to fill an altar with LP. Eventually, you'll build automated mob farms to fuel your growing empire of blood magic [citation:2].

The mod includes a full in-game guidebook (**Sanguine Scientiem**) that sequences the tiers and explains every mechanic. Budget a mob farm early — everything downstream drinks LP [citation:2].

**Key highlights:**
- ✅ **Six Blood Altar tiers** — Progressive magical power

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: What is Blood Magic?**
A: A sacrifice-driven magic mod where you convert your health and mob lives into Life Essence (LP), used for sigils, rituals, armor, and alchemy [citation:2].

**Q: Is Blood Magic free?**
A: Yes — 100% free. Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 [citation:5].

**Q: What Minecraft versions are supported?**
A: **1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1, and 1.21.1** (unofficial port). Support varies by version [citation:3][citation:17].

**Q: Forge, NeoForge, or Fabric?**
A: **Forge and NeoForge only.** No Fabric support.

**Q: How do I get LP without hurting myself?**
A: Build the **Well of Suffering** ritual over a mob pen feeding the altar. Incense Altars multiply self-sacrifice value [citation:2].

**Q: What are the Blood Altar tiers?**
A: Six tiers. Tier 3 unlocks Magician's Orb and serious sigils. Tier 5-6 host endgame orbs and rituals [citation:2][citation:16].

**Q: What is Living Armor?**
A: Armor that levels up upgrade tracks from how you play (sprinting, taking hits, digging). It becomes one of modded Minecraft's best armor sets by simply living in it [citation:2].

**Q: What is Demon Will?**
A: A parallel resource dropped from mobs killed with Sentient gear. Five types: Default, Corrosive, Destructive, Vengeful, Steadfast [citation:2][citation:8].

**Q: How do I view my LP?**
A: Craft a **Divination Sigil**. Right-click the altar to see its LP, or right-click away from it to see your soul network [citation:19].

**Q: Why am I dying so much?**
A: If you don't have enough LP for a task, it's taken from your health. Always check your essence levels before crafting [citation:19].

**Q: What is the Sanguine Scientiem?**
A: The in-game guidebook. It sequences tiers and explains everything. Read it [citation:5].

**Q: Does it work on Bedrock / mobile?**
A: No — **Java Edition only**.

**Q: How do I uninstall it?**
A: Delete the `.jar` from `.minecraft/mods/` and restart.

A: Contact us via [minemods.top](https://minemods.top/)
